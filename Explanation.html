The Problem

A document you can't read shouldn't get to decide your outcome.

Medical bills, insurance denial letters, contracts, written in language most people were never taught to read. Missed deadlines and misread fees aren't a small inconvenience, they're the actual cost. It hits hardest for people with less formal education and people more comfortable in a language other than English.

What Makes It Different

Any AI can summarize a document.

Ours is built for the person who wouldn't know to ask for one. No prompting skill required, no picking through a chatbot, just an upload button.
  • Structured output every time: risk flags, dates, actions, questions, not one flat paragraph.
  • Labels what the document clearly states versus what's being interpreted, so trust isn't just assumed.
  • A language dropdown does what typing "explain this in Telugu" would require someone to already know to ask for.
Built To Actually Work

Reliability was the hard part, not the AI.

  • A three-model fallback chain, so one AI provider being busy doesn't take the whole thing down.
  • Screen-reader support and keyboard navigation, tested, not assumed.
  • One-click PDF export that renders correctly in every supported language.
Beyond The Hackathon

Where this actually goes next.

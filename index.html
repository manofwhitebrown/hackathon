The Problem

Nobody teaches you to read a denial letter.

Medical bills, insurance letters, and contracts are written for professionals, not for the person holding them. Missed deadlines and misread fees aren't a small inconvenience, they have real financial and health consequences. It hits hardest for people with less formal education and people more comfortable in a language other than English.

What Makes It Different

Any AI can summarize a document. That was never the hard part.

The hard part is that the person who needs this most doesn't know how to ask a chatbot the right way.

A generic AI chatbot

  • Needs a well-written prompt to give a useful answer
  • Gives whatever it feels like, different every time
  • One flat paragraph, in whatever language you typed in
  • No distinction between fact and guess

Plain Terms

  • Upload a photo. That's the entire skill required
  • Same structured breakdown, every single time
  • A language dropdown, not a prompting skill
  • Labels what's stated versus what's inferred

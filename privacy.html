Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 2026

What Plain Terms does with your document

When you upload a document, it is sent directly to Google's Gemini API for analysis and is not saved anywhere by Plain Terms itself. We do not run a database, and we do not keep a copy of your file, your results, or your identity after your browser tab is closed.

What Google does with your document

Plain Terms uses Google's free-tier Gemini API to read and summarize your document. Under Google's free-tier terms, Google may use content sent to the API to improve its models. If you are uploading a document with sensitive personal, medical, financial, or legal information and are not comfortable with that, please do not use this tool, or remove identifying details first.

Review Google's own terms directly at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms for the current, authoritative version, since terms can change.

What we don't do

  • We don't create user accounts or require sign-in.
  • We don't use tracking cookies or sell your data.
  • We don't store uploaded files or analysis results on any server we control.

Accuracy and limitations

Plain Terms uses AI to explain documents in plain language. It can make mistakes, miss details, or misread unusual formatting, especially in low-quality photos or scans. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor, lawyer, or financial professional. Always confirm anything important, like deadlines, amounts owed, or medical or legal implications, with the relevant professional or the original document.

Acceptable use

Plain Terms is provided as a hackathon project, as-is, with no uptime or accuracy guarantees. Please don't use it to upload documents you don't have the right to share, or to attempt to overload or abuse the service.
